Bio

Now, as a Cyber Security undergrad at Texas A&M–San Antonio, I’m channeling my DevOps background into vulnerability assessment and threat hunting. This portfolio showcases my hands-on projects in DNS/email hardening, cloud security assessments, and OSINT toolkits. My goal is to bridge development and security by designing proactive defenses that protect critical assets.
